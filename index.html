Project Background & Challenge

Why we transitioned to a centralized preparation hub.

Previous Conditions

Operational Bottlenecks

  • Setup activities are still performed directly on the production line, disrupting regular operations.
  • High machine downtime rate during tool and material adjustment processes.
  • Repeated pallet trials due to the lack of centralized pre-production validation.
  • Long preparation lead times and difficulty in accurately monitoring pallet inventory.
* Resulting in cumulative daily output efficiency losses.
Modern Solution

CoS Setup Center Transformation

Establishing a dedicated centralized area for preparation, rigorous validation, systematic inventory management, and structured borrowing workflows.

Complete separation between setup areas and active production floors.
Digital tracking using integrated barcode identification systems.
Standardized pallet quality assurance prior to machine line entry.
Manufacturing Excellence Standard Initiative

Core Objectives

Strategic targets achieved through the implementation of the Setup Center system.

Reduce Production Downtime

Move pallet setup and validation activities from the production line to a CoS Setup Center, significantly minimizing machine downtime during model pallet setup.

Improve Production Readiness

Ensure every pallet is fully assembled, validated, and production-ready before delivery to the production line, enabling faster and smoother production startup.

Standardize Work Flow

Establish standardized pallet preparation, setup, and validation procedures to ensure consistent quality and reduce setup variations across all production lines.

Improve Pallet Traceability

Track every pallet through barcode identification, including preparation, borrowing, return, and current location for complete operational visibility.

Real-time Inventory Monitoring

Monitor pallet availability, storage location, borrowing status, and return records in real time through a centralized digital inventory management system.

Faster Model Implementation

Accelerate new model implementation by preparing and validating pallets in advance, allowing production lines to start with minimal setup time and fewer trial activities.
